# Hospitality, hotel and restaurant Kaizen - 2026-09-14

## Change

Enhanced the existing hospitality and restaurant accounting pack with explicit
revenue-centre, source-event, folio/POS, recipe/yield/waste, deposits/refunds,
tax-state, night-audit/day-close, reconciliation, dimension, idempotency and
reviewer requirements.

## Evidence discipline

Restaurant and hotel books supplied durable control concepts, not current rates,
law or accounting conclusions. Uganda EFRIS, privacy, payment and standards
claims remain source-register controlled and jurisdiction/reviewer dependent.
Every judgement, benchmark and statutory value needs source, period, scope,
confidence and a named review route.

## Experiment and gate

Hypothesis: a daily hospitality close pack tying PMS/POS/stock/payment/tax data
to the ledger will expose leakage earlier. Measure unreconciled items, duplicate
postings, unexplained variances and close-cycle time on an authorised pilot.
Rollback by reverting the overlay reference. Next review: 2026-10-14.
