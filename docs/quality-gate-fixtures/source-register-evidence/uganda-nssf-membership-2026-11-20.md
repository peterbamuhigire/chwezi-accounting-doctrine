## Evidence Captured

Official source owner: National Social Security Fund Uganda.

Verified facts, unchanged from the 2026-05-15 capture:

- Mandatory coverage applies to employers for employees "between 16 and 55 years of age, with the exception of employees under the Government Pensions scheme".
- The Fund covers "all employers, irrespective of the number of employees".
- Employer deducts 5 percent from the employee's total gross monthly wage.
- Employer adds 10 percent of the total gross monthly wage.
- Total mandatory contribution is 15 percent for each employee.
- Contributions are due by the 15th day of the following month.

The page shows no publication or last-updated date; its footer reads "© 2019-2021 NSSF Uganda". The body does not mention the NSSF (Amendment) Act 2022, which appears only as a navigation link. That link and the 2024 voluntary contributions regulation were not fetched.

## Scope Caveat

This archive supports only the mandatory contribution split, age band, exception and monthly due date. It does not verify benefit eligibility, voluntary contributions, amnesty, penalties, arrears, non-resident treatment, statutory amendments or payroll classification edge cases.
