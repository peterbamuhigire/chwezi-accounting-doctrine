# Accounting Full Kaizen Operation Prompt

Paste this prompt at the root of an accounting, reporting, reconciliation, control, tax, budget, or finance-system project.

## Configuration

```text
Entity/product and reporting purpose: [DISCOVER]
Jurisdiction, reporting basis, period, and currency: [DISCOVER]
Systems, ledgers, populations, and output locations: [DISCOVER]
Materiality, control owners, and reviewers: [DISCOVER]
Known exceptions, audit findings, or close pain points: [NONE OR LIST]
Cycle ID: [YYYY-MM-DD-short-name]
Improvement authority: project-local reversible edits are authorised; postings and external actions are not
```

## Prompt

Run a controller-grade Kaizen operation on this finance product. Inspect source evidence and the real workflow, freeze a capped baseline, remove waste without weakening control objectives, test each improvement through re-performance, standardise only proven changes, and leave a complete residual-risk handoff.

### Mandatory routing and boundaries

Read project instructions. Resolve Chwezi Accounting Doctrine and read its `README.md`, `doctrine/accounting-finance-doctrine.md`, applicable source register, finance quality/release gate, matched domain skills, and `skills/10-controls-governance-and-fraud/kaizen-engine-and-product-improvement/SKILL.md`. Read the Digital Research portfolio standard and verify current IFRS, tax, statutory, regulatory, and system claims through source evaluation and verification.

This prompt authorises read-only inspection and reversible project-local documentation, test, configuration, workbook-copy, and control-design edits. Do not post journals, reopen/close periods, alter production ledgers, submit returns, approve payments, accept residual risk, change live access, or modify canonical doctrine without explicit authority. Work on copies or safe fixtures. If reporting basis, population, source register, permissions, backup, rollback, segregation of duties, or reviewer authority is missing, stop execution and return a qualified plan. Use `NOT ASSESSED` for unavailable evidence.

### Cycle evidence

Create `docs/kaizen/<cycle-id>/` with `00-scope-and-evidence.md`, `01-baseline-scorecard.md`, `02-improvement-backlog.md`, `03-experiment-log.md`, `04-validation-record.md`, `05-final-report.md`, and `06-next-cycle.md`. Inventory reporting basis, policies, source registers, chart of accounts, subledgers, interfaces, transaction populations, reconciliations, close calendar, journals, controls, access roles, evidence retention, reports, exceptions, audit findings, system tests, reviewers, and release approvals.

### Capped baseline

Score ten equal dimensions with evidence, confidence, deficiency, and status:

1. Reporting purpose, authoritative basis, policies, source currency, and scope.
2. Transaction completeness, accuracy, occurrence, classification, cut-off, and evidence.
3. Chart/ledger invariants, subledger-to-GL integrity, interfaces, and posting rules.
4. Reconciliations, suspense, ageing, exceptions, re-performance, and close discipline.
5. Control design and operation, approvals, segregation of duties, period locks, and audit trail.
6. Tax, statutory, grant/donor, payroll, treasury, and regulatory treatment where applicable.
7. Financial statements, management reporting, budgets, forecasts, consolidation, and disclosures.
8. Finance-system configuration, migrations, automation, explainability, change control, and rollback.
9. Access, privacy, security, retention, fraud/AML signals, and incident handling.
10. Usability, workpapers, review evidence, residual risk, ownership, handoff, and next close cycle.

Show the raw calculation and publish `min(raw_overall, 65)`. Freeze it before improvement. Any misstatement, fraud, statutory, access, privacy, unreconciled material balance, or failed control remains a blocker regardless of score.

### Plan and experiment toward 95

Create a P0/P1/P2 backlog. For every action record assertion/control objective, evidence, root cause, exact artefact or process, hypothesis, owner and reviewer, measure, control guardrails, risk, smallest reversible change, rollback, stop rule, acceptance/re-performance evidence, residual risk, target contribution, and re-audit date.

Run one safe change at a time on a copy, test environment, or bounded process. Preserve the baseline. Re-perform the control, rebuild affected reports, reconcile upstream and downstream totals, test exception and failed paths, and compare cycle time or error reduction. Reject any improvement that saves time by weakening approval, period lock, audit trail, segregation, reconciliation, statutory verification, privacy, or reviewer accountability.

### Strict anti-AI-slop gate

Apply finance anti-slop controls throughout and run the audit after each major iteration and at release. Grade F blocks release. Any fabricated transaction, balance, source, standard, tax rate, reconciliation, approval, review, control test, audit evidence, or compliance claim is also a hard blocker. Reject generic control matrices, boilerplate accounting policies, unsupported IFRS/tax citations, invented journal entries, balancing plugs, unexplained rounding, hard-coded totals, silent overrides, fake reviewer sign-off, vague residual-risk language, and “automated” processes with no traceable system evidence.

Require every amount to reconcile to a source or labelled assumption; every posting rule to name the accounting basis and invariant; every control to state objective, owner, frequency, evidence, exception path, and reviewer; and every conclusion to separate preparation, review, assurance, and authority. Smooth prose and balanced totals cannot conceal an unsupported treatment or ineffective control.

### Validation

Run applicable doctrine validators, source-register checks, accounting invariants, formula/workbook audits, reconciliation and report rebuilds, access/SoD checks, migration tests, finance release gates, and independent controller review. Confirm opening plus movement equals closing, debits equal credits, subledgers reconcile to control accounts, statements cross-foot, cash and retained earnings roll forward, scenario assumptions are traceable, and exceptions retain owners. Record exact commands, samples, populations, reviewer sign-off, output paths, failures, and limitations.

### Standardise and re-measure

Promote accepted learning into the project policy, control matrix, reconciliation template, close checklist, fixture, automated test, runbook, evidence-retention rule, or reviewer checklist. Do not edit authoritative doctrine or accept risk without authority. Re-score from new evidence, state the uncapped final result, and retain all unresolved exceptions and `NOT ASSESSED` areas.

The final report must show before/after results, control impact, re-performance evidence, rolled-back trials, residual risk, release verdict, owner, and next review. Return the score summary, completed changes, validation record, blockers, evidence-pack path, and re-audit date. Never certify compliance or fairness without the required authority and evidence.
