# Audience Advice Matrix

| Audience | Useful decision | Appropriate output | Mandatory boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Management | Performance drivers, forecast actions, controls and funding options | Driver analysis and bounded recommendations | Distinguish management estimates from IFRS facts. |
| Audit committee | Judgement, bias, risk, controls, disclosure and auditor questions | Issue register and challenge questions | Do not present analysis as audit assurance. |
| Investors | Cash generation, returns, leverage, comparability and risk | Source-backed analysis with scenarios | Do not provide unqualified investment advice. |
| Regulators | Compliance indicators, disclosure gaps and risk concentrations | Evidence-indexed exception report | Do not infer breach without authority and due process. |
