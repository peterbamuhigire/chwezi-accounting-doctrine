# Book-driven Kaizen Wave 3: accounting application

Use this reference for accounting, controls, reporting, budgets, reconciliations, finance
systems, and automation.

Owning skill: [Kaizen owner](../SKILL.md).

## Transfer controls

- Give every metric a definition, source lineage, time basis, transformation, owner, and
  reconciliation or invariant. Dashboard summaries must expose exceptions and context.
- Treat improvement as risk reduction: baseline the control, identify root cause, run one
  reversible change, re-perform the control, retain evidence, and assign residual-risk ownership.
- Use Git-style traceability for models and rules: reviewable snapshots, approvals, conflict
  handling, rollback, and separation of preparation from approval/posting.
- For agents or scripts, enforce least privilege, bounded inputs/actions, human approval for
  consequential postings, alerting, circuit breakers, and a tested recovery path.

## Boundaries

The books do not establish IFRS, tax, statutory, audit, or control requirements. Current
accounting claims must use Chwezi doctrine and authoritative source registers. Do not infer
control effectiveness from a polished dashboard or fabricated/tidy data; mark missing evidence
`NOT_ASSESSED`.

## Measures and routing

Track reconciliation breaks, exception ageing, close cycle time, re-performance pass rate,
adjustment/error rate, lineage completeness, approval segregation, automation rollback, and
residual risk. Route to the applicable reporting, close, controls, fraud, and source-register
skills.

## Currentness gate

Verify current IFRS/tax/statutory, security, technology, and lifecycle claims at task time;
record source/date/freshness/support/uncertainty and review triggers.
