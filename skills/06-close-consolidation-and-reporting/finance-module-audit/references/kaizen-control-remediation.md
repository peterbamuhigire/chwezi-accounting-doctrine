# Kaizen Control Remediation Loop

Use after a close exception, reconciliation break, audit finding, control failure, system change, or recurring manual workaround.

1. Preserve the evidence and determine whether the issue is a misstatement, control deficiency, process waste, data-quality issue, or user-training gap.
2. Establish the baseline: population, period, monetary exposure, frequency, root cause, control objective, and residual risk.
3. Design the smallest countermeasure that removes waste without removing the control objective, segregation of duties, approval, audit trail, or statutory requirement.
4. Re-perform, run invariants, rebuild the report, obtain independent review, and test the failure path.
5. Standardise the fix in the doctrine, mapping, control, runbook, test, and training record; retain residual risk and next review.

No book or Lean principle overrides the finance doctrine or current source register.
