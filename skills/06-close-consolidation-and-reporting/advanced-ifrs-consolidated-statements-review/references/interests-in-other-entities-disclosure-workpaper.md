## Required cross-checks

- Ownership and voting rights agree to legal/entity records.
- Control, joint-control or significant-influence conclusion agrees to the
  consolidation scope memo.
- Summarised financial information and NCI information tie to source statements.
- Restrictions on cash, assets, liabilities or distributions are disclosed.
- Commitments, guarantees, support and exposure to losses are captured.
- Effects on position, performance and cash flows reconcile to the group pack.
