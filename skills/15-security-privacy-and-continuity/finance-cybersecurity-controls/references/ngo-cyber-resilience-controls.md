# Civil-Society Cyber-Resilience Controls for Finance

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Stéphane Duguin,
*Cybersecurity for NGOs: Attack Prevention and Threat Response*.

## Finance control translation

Apply the NGO resilience cycle to finance systems: board accountability and
mission priorities; enablement through training and trusted support; identity,
data, domain, and dependency identification; least privilege, MFA, secrets,
backups, and secure administration; useful detection; and evidence-ready
response and recovery.

Prioritise payment, payroll, donor, beneficiary, and reporting paths by harm.
Preserve incident evidence, segregation of duties, approval records, and source
documents. Restore in a staged environment, rotate credentials, reconcile to the
ledger, and obtain the required reviewer sign-off before resuming posting.
