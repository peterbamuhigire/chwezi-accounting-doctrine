# Book-driven value, cash, and working capital

This reference is an independent synthesis of durable principles from the supplied corporate-finance, valuation, business-system, supply-chain, franchise, and improvement books.

## Decision model

Separate accounting profit, cash flow, free cash flow, liquidity, and economic value. Start with the decision and horizon, then identify the statements, operating drivers, assumptions, scenario range, and control evidence needed to support it.

## Quality and stewardship

Assess business quality through customer value, competitive position, repeatability, management behaviour, capital allocation, debt capacity, and resilience. Treat qualitative judgements as hypotheses with evidence, not as a replacement for reconciliation or analysis.

## Working-capital and network lens

Connect receivables, inventory, payables, lead time, service level, capacity, and cash conversion. Test demand, price, margin, delays, stock-outs, capex, and financing sensitivity. Name the operating owner and the trigger for intervention.

## Currentness gate

Do not use books as authority for current IFRS/IAS requirements, tax rules, rates, regulatory policy, or software behaviour. Route current accounting and reporting claims through the accounting doctrine and Digital Research source-verification gates. For this wave, IFRS 18's effective date and related claims are controlled by the currentness register; verify the official IFRS Foundation source before use.

## Control discipline

Preserve immutable posted history, document estimates and revisions, reconcile source-to-report, and distinguish management scenarios from reported facts. Every model should expose assumptions, formulas, sensitivities, and limitations.
